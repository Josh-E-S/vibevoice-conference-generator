How Chorus works

VibeVoice generates expressive, long-form, multi-speaker conversational audio from text. It uses continuous speech tokenizers at an ultra-low 7.5 Hz frame rate and a next-token diffusion framework to synthesize up to 90 minutes of speech with up to 4 distinct speakers.

  • Multi-speaker — up to 4 distinct voices in one take
  • Long-form — up to 90 minutes of continuous audio
  • Natural flow — turn-taking, filler words, natural pacing
  • Two model sizes — Fast (1.5B) for iteration, Best (7B) for fidelity
